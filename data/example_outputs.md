# Illustrative output examples

These are target-style examples written for the application. They are not
transcripts extracted from the source report.

## Missing acknowledgement

**New input:** A vehicle reports an obstruction, but the traffic signal has
not received the report.

- **Participants:** Vehicle and traffic signal.
- **Purpose:** Information seeking.
- **Exchange:** “Can you confirm receiving my obstruction report?” / “I have
  not confirmed receipt. Please repeat what you observed and where.”
- **Why:** The input explicitly says the signal has not received the report.
  The immediate communication goal is to obtain or confirm information.
- **Unknown:** The exact location and independent verification of the
  obstruction.

## Shared action after confirmation

**New input:** A vehicle and signal have confirmed an obstruction. They need
to decide how to coordinate a warning for approaching traffic.

- **Participants:** Vehicle and traffic signal.
- **Purpose:** Deliberation.
- **Exchange:** “The report is confirmed. What warning plan should we use?” /
  “Let us agree on a message and who will send it.”
- **Why:** The report is confirmed, and the stated goal is to choose a shared
  action.
- **Unknown:** Who has authority to issue messages in this setting.

## Competing interest

**New input:** Two vehicles both want the same parking space and need to
settle who may use it.

- **Participants:** Two vehicles.
- **Purpose:** Negotiation.
- **Exchange:** “I need the available space. Can we agree who takes it?” /
  “Let us clarify our positions and agree on an order.”
- **Why:** The vehicles have competing interests and seek a settlement.
- **Unknown:** Any priority rule that applies to the space.
